## Question and hypothesis (H3)

Where does a unit of compute create deeper lasting transformation: in conversational and media consumer products, or in discovery loops coupled to physical laws? This paper tests the portfolio’s third core hypothesis (**H3**): *“Scientific AI will drive deeper structural change than consumer AI”* across its four natural subtopics: **materials discovery, therapeutics (abstract computational triage), climate and physics**.

High-accuracy protein-structure prediction with AlphaFold showed that when a domain possesses rigorous geometric constraints and an experimental validation benchmark, deep learning compresses decades of structural characterization.<Nota f="jumper-2021" /> The 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry explicitly recognized this shift toward computational protein design and structure prediction.<Nota f="kva-nobel-2024" /> At the same time, the International AI Safety Report 2026 emphasizes that accelerating *in-silico* candidate generation is neither identical to confirmed physical deployment nor free of dual-use governance risks.<Nota f="iasr-2026" /> Meanwhile, physical Earth-system assessments by the IPCC<Nota f="ipcc-ar6-wg1" /> and International Energy Agency projections of rising data-centre electricity demand through 2030<Nota f="aie-ia-2026" /> raise a sharper allocation question: which uses of global compute generate compounding physical returns that justify their energy footprint?

## Verifiable-oracle theory and Amdahl’s Law of discovery

The structural asymmetry between **scientific AI** and **consumer AI** rests on two formal mechanisms implemented in `experiments/h3-ia-cientifica-vs-consumer/model.ts`:

1. **Oracle fidelity and non-rival compounding:** Consumer AI optimizes against subjective human preferences and competes for an inelastic daily attention ceiling (`24` hours), facing concave saturation (`3.2 × E(t) / (2.2 + E(t))`) and rapid competitive rent erosion. Scientific AI searches vast combinatorial spaces filtered by **verifiable physical or formal oracles** (`q_oracle ∈ [0.76, 0.93]`): conservation laws, density-functional simulations or controlled assays. Validated breakthroughs accumulate as **non-rival knowledge capital** that raises the productivity of subsequent discovery cycles.
2. **Amdahl’s Law in hybrid physical pipelines:** An end-to-end discovery cycle combines an *in-silico* screening share `(1 − β_phys)` accelerated by `S(t)` with a physical synthesis or clinical validation share `β_phys` scaling more slowly at rate `V(t)`:

`E(t) = 1 / (((1 − β_phys) / S(t)) + (β_phys / V(t)))`

Once `S(t)` outpaces `V(t)`, end-to-end pipeline speedup is upper-bounded by `V(t) / β_phys` and cycle time concentrates inside physical validation.

## Experimental design and quantitative findings

We simulate six domains across `10` years (`2026–2035`) and `25` deterministic seeds (`1,500` SHA-256-verified rows): four verifiable-oracle domains (`materials-discovery`, `therapeutics-preclinical-triage`, `climate-earth-systems`, `fundamental-physics`) and two consumer baselines (`consumer-assistant`, `digital-media`). The table summarizes `2035` means:

| Domain | Class | Speedup `E(2035)` | Physical share | Surplus / PFLOP-day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Materials discovery | Scientific oracle | 8.25× | 88.9% | 15.43 |
| Fundamental physics | Scientific oracle | 7.80× | 80.5% | 13.16 |
| Climate & Earth systems | Scientific oracle | 9.39× | 81.8% | 12.86 |
| Preclinical triage (R10) | Scientific oracle | 4.38× | 93.6% | 8.88 |
| Consumer assistant | Human attention | 13.09× | 82.7% | 1.18 |
| Digital media | Human attention | 17.58× | 81.9% | 0.92 |

Three analytical findings support and bound **H3**:

1. **Wide divergence in structural return per unit of compute:** Although consumer products exhibit faster raw iteration speedups (`13.09×–17.58×`), the four verifiable-oracle domains generate **`7.5×` to `16.8×` higher cumulative structural surplus per PFLOP-day** (`8.88–15.43` versus `0.92–1.18`).
2. **Migration of the binding bottleneck into physical validation:** By `2035`, between `80.5%` (physics) and `93.6%` (preclinical triage, where `β_phys = 0.52` reflects clinical validation duration) of total cycle time sits inside physical verification. Scaling *in-silico* models further without automating synthesis and experimental characterization hits sharp Amdahl diminishing returns.
3. **Synthesis across H1, H2 and H3:** Unlocking scientific AI’s full surplus requires autonomous laboratory and verification infrastructure (**H1**) governed by verifiable audit and biosafety commitments (**H2**).

## Falsifiability criterion and limitations

**What would falsify H3:** The hypothesis would be falsified if by `2035` verifiable multifactor productivity gains and net economic surplus attributable to AI-assisted breakthroughs in materials, energy, climate and biomedicine fell below those generated by consumer assistants and generative media.

**Limitations:** Compute intensities and unit structural values are stylized comparative indices rather than constant-dollar national accounts. In strict adherence to policy R10, no molecular design or operational biology is represented.
