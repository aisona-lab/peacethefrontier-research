Peace The Frontier is the research and engineering portfolio of **Carlos Nicolás Soto**, organized around a central thesis: **autonomous agents as the operational, institutional and scientific infrastructure of the next decade (2026–2036)**. The site connects three complementary layers: original working papers with linked code and data in [Research](/en/research/), reproducible synthetic environments in [Sim](/en/sim/) and [Open](/en/open/), and the 17-topic historical and futures context in the [Atlas](/en/atlas/).

## Who researches and publishes

**Carlos Nicolás Soto** formulates the hypotheses, builds the mathematical models and computational simulations, and stands behind every published claim, source and evidence grade. Whenever AI agents assist with code, literature cross-checks or bilingual editing, that assistance is disclosed in the publication header. Passing automated checks never substitutes for human sign-off before removing draft status. Full details are in the [methodology](/en/methodology/). If this work is useful to you, you can [support the research](/en/support/).

## Thesis and scientific identity

<PuntoDeVista lang="en">

I believe much of the public conversation around artificial intelligence is looking at the wrong layer: we keep benchmarking conversational chatbots while the real structural transition is toward agentic loops that execute long-horizon workflows, allocate shared resources and run scientific pipelines.

My research programme tests three falsifiable hypotheses: that replacing copilots with autonomous agents within 5–10 years hinges on verification infrastructure rather than raw model size (**H1**); that superintelligence is fundamentally a multi-agent coordination and Goodhart-incentive problem (**H2**); and that AI coupled to verifiable physical oracles will create deeper structural value than consumer AI (**H3**). Every claim should stand on explicit equations, open code and clear conditions that could prove it wrong.

</PuntoDeVista>

## Commitments to readers

- **Original research with public code:** every core hypothesis is backed by executable TypeScript models in `experiments/`, `SHA-256`-verified datasets and direct GitHub links.
- **Explicit falsifiability:** every paper states which future empirical observations would refute its hypothesis.
- **Graded evidence and separated opinion:** consensus, emerging evidence, speculative hypotheses and conditional scenarios are visually distinct; authorial opinion is always isolated.
- **Strict sensitive-content policy (R10):** biology, weapons and surveillance are addressed exclusively through governance, history and abstract aggregate metrics—never operational protocols.
- **Privacy and bilingual parity:** no advertising or tracking cookies; every investigation is published with identical rigour in Spanish and English.

## Conflict of interest disclosure

**Carlos Nicolás Soto** designs and builds software products powered by autonomous agents at **aisona-lab**. This represents a **direct professional and financial conflict of interest**: the author holds a stake in autonomous agents becoming reliable, useful and widely adopted as infrastructure.

To counter this bias transparently:

- All simulations driven by author-selected assumptions (`H1`, `H2` and `H3`) are explicitly classified as **Scenario** at `v0.x`, never as established empirical evidence.
- The `H1` interactive explorable allows any technical reader to perturb the parameters and verify exactly where the conclusion reverses.
- Empirical measurements from open repositories and agent logs ([/en/research/h1-data/](/en/research/h1-data/)) will be published in full even—and especially—when they contradict `H1`.
- Build validation prevents any hypothesis paper from reaching `v1.0.0` without empirical calibration and published comments from at least two external technical reviewers.

## Direction, agents and sustainability

The lead researcher defines the questions, modelling assumptions and validation gates. Agents assist with literature retrieval, code verification and controlled translation. Independent portfolio status does not claim university affiliation or external peer review unless explicitly stated. Any future support or collaboration is disclosed without altering findings or version histories.
